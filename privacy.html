jimmy mack revenue - Privacy policy

jimmy mack revenue is used only by the owner of this YouTube channel.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the estimated revenue figures in YouTube Analytics for the channel that signs in. It has read-only access and cannot change anything on the channel or the Google account.

How the information is used

The figures are shown only to the channel owner. They are not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

jimmy mack revenue's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

The figures and the sign-in pass are kept on the owner's private server, only to show the revenue.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
